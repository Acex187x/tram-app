# Cab and rear geometry audit — 5 September 2026

These are observations of photographs, not certified CAD measurements. References
are research material and are not imported into the application. Original fleet
gallery URLs and credits are in `../sources.json`. New sources are listed below.

## T3, T3R.P and T3R.PLF

- **All three have one broad central front windscreen and two narrow corner
  panes.** Two wipers do not imply two main panes. A vertical cream strip through
  the middle of the windscreen is incorrect. The gasket posts are out toward the
  corners, with a continuous large pane between them. See `../t3-01.jpg`,
  `../t3rp-03.jpg` (8493), `../t3rplf-01.jpg` (8284), and
  `t3plf-front-assembly-dpp.png` (8284 under construction).
- The central front face is broad, with rounded side corners; it is not a fully
  semicircular panoramic glass cylinder. Upper cream brow is substantial, rounded
  at its ends and broad across the middle. A loft that collapses the entire front
  roof to one apex makes the wrong silhouette. The main roof has a broad rounded
  cross-section, joining that cap without a triangular crease.
- Front windscreen slopes back while the red nose bulges forward. There is a
  distinct cream ledge below the gasket. The lamp fascia is high enough for two
  separated round chrome headlights; vertical corner indicator clusters are
  distinct from those headlights. Horizontal chrome bumper wraps around both
  corners, above the deep pale skirt and real coupling opening.
- PLF 8284 has a conspicuous central downward cream **V** into the upper red
  nose. Its cab roof equipment, black gasket joint between end cap and side
  shell, and extended body distinguish it from RP. Do not transfer every detail
  from 8286: the latter has a single front wiper in the DPP photograph, while
  8284 has dual wipers.
- PLF passenger windows, excluding cab/corner panes: **left 3 high + 3 low +
  3 high = 9; right 3 between front/middle doors and 3 between middle/rear**.
  Immediately either side of the middle door are the two lowered right windows.
  `../t3rplf-04.jpg` gives a near-side-on left view. The low group spans roughly
  4 m around the body center; exact pane centers are not dimensioned in the
  available primary drawings. With nine windows, a centered sill drop predicate
  near ±2.1 m is a reasonable photo fit. Regular RP has eight left windows.
- PLF rear, `t3plf-rear-dpp.png`: one broad central pane plus narrow corner panes,
  nearly vertical, with one **top-mounted rear wiper**. Red rises directly toward
  the window sill; no front cream V or destination eyebrow. Small rectangular
  lamps sit low on the red panel, corner vertical clusters are separate, and
  small round reflectors sit below the chrome bumper. Two round front-like
  taillamps halfway up the panel are wrong for this representative. Horizontal
  lines visible through the glass in the delivery photograph are transport
  framing/interior structure, not extra glass mullions.
- Historic 6102 and RP rear-specific lamp positions have not been confirmed by
  an equally sharp operator rear photograph. Do not silently apply every PLF
  rear detail to both variants.

## KT8D5.RN2P

`../kt8d5-05.jpg` (9102) is a sharp operator front view. Both ends are driving
cabs. A broad center pane is flanked by narrow corner panes inside the black
surround. Display occupies the upper central glass area. Lower face is flat with
angled corners, unlike the round T3 nose. The thin cream band below the black
window mask, red lamp belt, straight bumper and two outer large/two inner small
round lamps are essential. The coupler interrupts the bumper/skirt centrally.
Retain the mirrored driving-cab structure at the second end.

## 14T

`14t-pid-2021-1.jpg` is a close low-angle primary front view. Tall curved central
glass tapers inward at the top; narrow body pillars and separate side cab panes
must remain visible. A single long lower-mounted wiper crosses the glass. Three
round lamps per side occupy a recessed vertical panel, not three bare circles on
an undifferentiated smooth shell. There is a broad horizontal bumper seam and
small lower oval lamps. Side elevation has a long backward-sloping windscreen
above a projecting lower nose, with a flat-ish lower skirt.

The primary four-view drawing in `14t-praha-four-views.png` confirms related
front/rear shells. Its rear display is small and centered, rather than the full
front destination display. Rear should show passenger space, not driver controls.
A sharp operator rear photograph remains missing: the four-view drawing is the
basis for rear shape, so small rear fittings remain lower-confidence.

Rigid/gap fit in `orthographic-measurements.json` is consistent with bogies in
sections 1/3/5 and passenger doors in 2/4/5 (2/2/1). **Rear passenger doorway is
single width, roughly 0.65 m**, unlike the 1.30 m double doors in suspended
sections. Front single doorway is driver access. Front-to-rear door centers and
bogie centers in that JSON remain consistent with physical shell boundaries.

## 15T

`../15t-03.jpg` is the representative original-generation 9286 front. Broad glass
has rounded upper corners, a full-width destination display inside the upper
black mask, and narrow lateral pillars. Black fleet-number band below the glass,
silver belt holding three lamps per side in dark rectangular recesses, red lower
bumper with a second pair of dark inset lamp groups. Lamp spacing is unequal;
outer main round headlamp is larger. Single lower-mounted wiper is essential.

`15t-rear-photographer.jpg` is a very sharp **later-generation 9389**, from the
original photographer. It establishes rear route-only display, tall passenger
window, one long lower-mounted rear wiper, white/silver lamp belt and red lower
bumper. Its sculpted teardrop lamp housings belong to that generation; do not
replace 9286's original lamp housings with them without matching evidence.

## 52T

`52t-rear-dpp.png` is an explicit rear photograph of 9501 in DPP's factory report.
The related front and rear shell is supported, but the rear has a small centered
route-only display and visible passenger space. Rear lamp capsules contain a
large outer red marker and smaller inner element, not three identical white
headlights. No rear wiper is clearly visible in this photo; do not invent its
geometry. The lower PID red stripe is **off center**: front-right/rear-left in
head-on image coordinates. The rest of the lower face is pale gray/white.

Front `../52t-06.jpg` and the February factory report show a broad rounded mask,
a large tall central glass area, display above, and a narrower black lamp band
immediately below. Roof top is broad with curved corners; a pointed dome is wrong.
Horizontal lamp capsules have an outside main circular lamp and inner vertical
LED elements. A curved seam separates lamp band from pale lower nose; fine access
panel seams and fasteners help recognition more than generic extra protrusions.

Manufacturer elevation fit is internally consistent: 9/3.5/3.79/3.5/9 m rigid
shells + four 0.8 m bellows = 31.99 m. Bogies at 2.30/11.56/20.43/29.69 m occupy
sections 1/2/4/5; no bogie in middle section. Doors lie in sections 1/3/5.
These fits measure visible physical shell boundaries, not articulation centers.
