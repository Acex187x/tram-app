Motion-log analysis of `session-2026-07-11.jsonl` (20,523 records, 11.1 min,
182 trams) against the interpolation engine described in
`docs/decisions/interpolation-engine.md`. Analysis only — no engine code
changed.

**Reproduce:**

```
python3 docs/calibration/analyze.py   # hygiene, error, speed, paceBias tables
python3 docs/calibration/replay.py    # coarse 1D replay of candidate constants
```

Both scripts take an optional JSONL path argument and print plain-text tables;
every number below comes from one of them.

## 2. What devM actually measures — the staleness floor

`devM = |simDist − obsDist|` compares the sim **now** against a fix that is on
average ~28 s old (half the 55 s refresh interval). A *perfect* sim sitting
exactly on the real tram would still show
`devM ≈ v_real × fix_age ≈ 5.9 m/s × 27 s ≈ 160 m` at the median. Observed
devM p50 is **130 m** — i.e. the smooth sim is already *inside* the naive
staleness floor. devM in this log is dominated by fix staleness, not sim error.

The sawtooth confirms it: devM p50 is **74 m right after a fix refresh** and
**249 m just before the next** — growth ≈ 3.2 m/s ≈ the median real tram
speed. The sim moves correctly while the logged fix stands still.

**The honest quality metric** is the prediction error *at the moment a fresh
fix arrives* (prev poll's position − new obsDist, n=1552):

| | signed p50 | \|err\| p50 | \|err\| p90 | % ahead of fresh fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| smooth sim | **−38 m** | **86 m** | 260 m | **27.1%** |
| projSim (live) | −2 m | 73 m | 201 m | 41.4% |

The smooth sim trails fresh reality by ~38 m median (TRAIL_M=10 + controller
lag + projection underestimate) and is ahead only 27% of the time — the
"ride behind reality" design goal is mostly working. projSim is nearly
unbiased and ~15% more accurate in absolute terms, as expected for a
dead-reckoner that re-seeds on every fix.

Raw devM tables (still useful for relative comparisons):

- overall: p50 130, p75 266, p90 413, p95 517, p99 771, max 2384
- signed sim−obs(stale): +120 p50; sim "ahead of last fix" 74.5% of records —
  almost entirely the staleness artifact above.
- by mode: cruise p50 160; **dwell p50 2.0** (sim dwelling = usually parked on
  the fix); **terminal p50 325, mean 371** ← real failure mode, see §5.
- by model: t3rplf/t3rp best (signed mean 128/137), 15t/52t worst (177/180) —
  confounded with lines (15t dominates long fast radials 22/17/9); no
  model-specific action supported by this data.
- worst lines by signed mean: **15 (+242), 31 (+228), 5 (+196), 22 (+187)**;
  best: 16 (+105), 3 (+118), 2 (+123).

## 3. Speed reality vs the profile

Real speeds from consecutive obsDist advances (Δobs/Δt, 4 s ≤ Δt ≤ 120 s,
n=1437 — note: intervals ~55 s usually *include dwell time*, so these are
conservative for pure cruise):

| km/h | p50 | p75 | p90 | p95 | share above cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all | 21.3 | 30.2 | 41.3 | 49.4 | 4.6% > 50 |
| centre bbox | **18.0** | 24.9 | **32.5** | 41.4 | 11.5% > 31 |
| outside | **23.0** | 32.0 | **42.9** | 50.6 | — |

- The user's prediction is confirmed: outskirts run faster (p50 23.0 vs 18.0,
  p90 42.9 vs 32.5 km/h).
- **The 50 km/h network default is far above typical reality**: implied pace
  ratio outside = 23/50 ≈ **0.46** at the median; even p90 (42.9) is below the
  cap. The cap is right as a *cap* (only 4.6% of intervals exceed it) but as a
  cruise *target* it is ~2× too fast.
- The centre 31 km/h cap is roughly correct as a cap (p90 32.5) — but it was
  **inactive** during this night session, so this dataset cannot validate the
  daytime zone logic itself.
- Per-line medians cluster tightly at 18–28 km/h (17: 24.0, 20: 27.9,
  5/18/21: ~18) — line-level differences exist but are second-order vs the
  global gap to the 50 km/h reference.

### paceBias validation

The engine already knows all this — that's what paceBias learned:

- distribution (excluding the 9.7% of records at the fresh-sim value 1.00):
  p50 **0.58**, mean 0.62; histogram (0.1 bins): 0.4→1361, 0.5→5660, 0.6→5568,
  0.7→2181, 0.8→1687, 0.9→1273, 1.0→710, 1.1→87.
- **7.3% of converged values sit ≤0.45**, i.e. at/near the per-sample clamp
  floor `PACE_BIAS_MIN_RATIO=0.4` — the clamp binds for the slowest trams.
  Nothing approaches the 1.6 ceiling (max observed 1.1).
- mean bias by minute is flat (0.612 → 0.629) — the fleet was already
  converged when logging started; the session shows steady state, not
  convergence dynamics.
- paceBias median 0.58 > raw ratio 0.46 because the engine deducts scheduled
  dwell from the interval (and its expected speed includes curve caps < 13.9).

**The cost of the 1.0 prior, measured directly** — at-fix prediction error
split by the sim's bias state at that moment:

| bias state | n | signed p50 | \|err\| p50 | \|err\| p90 | % ahead |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fresh (bias = 1.00) | 96 | **+25.5** | 101 | **385** | **56%** |
| converged ≤ 0.7 | 1048 | −42 | 81.5 | 235 | 25% |
| converged > 0.7 | 409 | −47 | 98 | 300 | 26% |

Fresh sims sprint at profile pace (~2× reality), overshoot ahead of the real
tram (56% ahead vs the 25% design norm), and produce the worst tail (p90 385 m).
With fixes 55 s apart and half-life 150 s, α per fix ≈ 0.22 → convergence from
1.0 to ~0.65 takes ~4–5 fixes ≈ **4–5 minutes** of visible misbehavior per new
trip/teleport.

## 4. Coarse 1D replay of candidate constants

`replay.py` re-runs the engine's pace controller (regimes, clamps, teleports
verbatim) over each tram's logged fix sequence. Coarse by construction — no
stops/dwells/schedule anchor; the observation is projected forward at a
trailing EWMA of the tram's real speed. Its baseline is therefore *worse* than
the real engine (at-fix |err| p50 136 vs logged 86; %ahead 60 vs 27) — read
**deltas between configs**, not absolute values.

| config | \|err\| p50 | \|err\| p90 | signed p50 | %ahead | devM p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A baseline (current constants) | 136 | 387 | +50 | 60.5% | 254 |
| B prior 0.6 | **126** | **351** | +25 | 55.3% | 232 |
| C caps 42/29 km/h | 134 | 376 | +43 | 58.9% | 248 |
| D caps 42/29 + prior 0.75 | 127 | 357 | +28 | 56.6% | 235 |
| E TRAIL_M 25 | 132 | 381 | +36 | 57.3% | 242 |
| F half-life 60 s | 134 | 387 | +43 | 59.0% | 247 |
| G clamp_lo 0.3 | 136 | 387 | +48 | 59.9% | 253 |
| H = D + clamp_lo 0.3 | 126 | 357 | +25 | 56.3% | 234 |
| I = D + TRAIL 20 + hl 60 | 128 | 366 | +24 | 55.3% | 229 |

The replay agrees with the logged-data split: **the prior dominates**; cap
changes and half-life barely move steady-state error (paceBias re-normalizes
whatever cap you pick — ratio = real/expected — so after convergence the
product `cap × bias` is invariant). The prior is the one constant the EWMA
cannot fix retroactively.

## 5. Ranked findings & root causes

1. **paceBias prior = 1.0 on a network whose converged bias is ~0.58**
   (P1, biggest lever). Every new sim (new trip, teleport — 9.7% of records)
   spends its first ~4–5 min simulating a tram ~1.7× too fast: 56% ahead at
   fix refresh, |err| p90 385 m, then hard-brake crawls when the next fix
   lands behind it — exactly the sprint-and-crawl artifact paceBias was built
   to remove, still present at every sim birth.
2. **Terminal latch runs ahead and sticks** (P1). 450 records (2.2%) across 37
   trams sit in `terminal` with signed sim−obs p50 **+324 m** (p90 560): the
   sim (often freshly created, bias=1) sprints to the last stop / geometry
   end, latches `phase='terminal'`, `v=0`, and waits — while the real tram is
   still hundreds of meters out. Errors <500 m never teleport, so it stays
   wrong until the real tram arrives or the trip swaps. Highest per-mode
   error in the log (mean 371 m vs cruise 203).
3. **AVL fixes are ~55 s apart, not ~20 s** (P2, environmental). All
   prediction horizons are ~3× longer than designed for; `STALE_AFTER_MS=90s`
   sits *below* the p90 fix interval (95 s) — verify that staleness is keyed
   off feed presence, not fix freshness, or live trams get dropped/respawned
   (each respawn re-triggers finding 1).
4. **The 50 km/h cruise reference is ~2× real median pace** (P2). Mostly
   masked by converged paceBias; still the multiplier on every fresh sim's
   error and on the bold-catch-up sprint speed (1.5 × 13.9 × bias).
5. **Sample clamp floor 0.4 binds** for the slowest ~7% of trams (P3) — they
   cannot learn how slow they really are.
6. **devM as logged is not a sim-quality metric** (P3, observability): it is
   dominated by fix staleness (floor ≈ 160 m). The at-fix-refresh probe had to
   be reconstructed offline; log it directly.

## 6. Concrete proposals (constants/formulas + expected effect from this data)

Ordered by measured impact per unit of risk:

**R1 — paceBias prior 0.62, and inherit it across teleports.**
`createSim: paceBias = PACE_BIAS_PRIOR = 0.62` (session fleet median 0.58–0.63;
round to 0.6 if you prefer). On teleport, reset toward the *prior*, not 1.0 —
or better, keep a per-`key` last-known bias in `TramEngine` and re-seed new
sims for the same vehicle from it (drivers don't change at teleports).
*Expected (measured):* fresh-sim at-fix signed p50 +25.5 → ≈ −40 (converged
behavior), |err| p90 385 → ≈ 235 (−39%), %ahead 56 → ~25%; replay: −7% global
|err| p50, −9% p90, %ahead −5 pts. Also directly shrinks finding 2 (terminal
overshoot is mostly fresh-sim sprint).

**R2 — un-latch terminal when the observation contradicts it.**
In `tick`/`applySnapshot`: if `phase==='terminal'` and a fresh obs projects
> `TERMINAL_HOLD_TOLERANCE_M ≈ 150 m` *behind* the latched position, don't
hold — treat like a normal cruise sim (the envelope re-brakes it at the end
anyway). Alternatively: only latch terminal when
`obsDist > totalM − 250` in the first place.
*Expected (measured):* the 450 terminal records' signed p50 +324 m → bounded
by the tolerance (~150 m), i.e. roughly −50% error on 2.2% of records and the
single worst per-mode mean (371 m) drops to ≈ cruise levels.

**R3 — lower the cruise reference, keep the safety cap.**
Introduce `V_CRUISE_REF_MS = 11.7` (42 km/h ≈ outside p90 real speed) used by
`cruiseCapAt` as the pace-controller reference, while `vAllowedAt` keeps
`V_MAX_MS = 13.9` for the braking envelope (caps observed exceeded by only
4.6% of intervals). Centre value: leave 8.6 — this night session never
exercised it (see §7).
*Expected:* replay −1.5% |err| p50 alone, but it bounds worst-case fresh-sim
sprint speed by 16% and composes with R1 (config D/H). Low risk: converged
bias re-normalizes (learned values rise ~19%, e.g. 0.58 → 0.69, safely inside
the clamp).

**R4 — widen the sample clamp floor: `PACE_BIAS_MIN_RATIO 0.4 → 0.3`.**
Frees the ~7% of trams pinned at ≤0.45. Replay-neutral globally (config G) but
targeted at the slowest vehicles' sprint-and-crawl. If R3 ships, the floor
binds less anyway (ratios rise); still cheap insurance.

**R5 — keep `TRAIL_M = 10`; do not raise it.** At fix refresh the smooth sim
already trails by 38 m median; %ahead is 27%. Raising TRAIL to 25 buys only
−3 pts %ahead in replay while adding 15 m of permanent lag on top of 38.
The ahead-tail is a *fresh-sim* problem (56% ahead) — R1 fixes the cause.

**R6 — keep `PACE_BIAS_HALF_LIFE_S = 150`.** Replay shows hl=60 is
steady-state neutral, and with R1 there is no cold start left to accelerate;
150 s retains more smoothing against the noisy 55 s fix cadence. Revisit only
if driver-swap adaptation proves too slow in longer sessions.

**R7 — observability: make the log self-sufficient.** Add to
`CalibrationRecord`: `obsAtMs` (true fix timestamp → real latency, not the
poll-quantized proxy), `target` (pace-controller target at t), and a
`teleport` flag. Then `analyze.py`'s at-fix probe becomes exact and dwell/
schedule effects become measurable. Costs ~20 bytes/record.

## 7. Not enough data to conclude

- **Daytime centre-zone cap (8.6 m/s)** — session was 23:09–23:21; the zone
  cap was off. Centre-vs-outside real-speed split above used geography only.
  Needs a daytime session before touching `V_CENTER_MS`.
- **Dwell defaults (18±8 s)** — fix cadence (55 s) ≫ dwell duration and the
  log has no stop table; real dwell lengths are unobservable here. The dwell
  deduction inside `updatePaceBias` is likewise unvalidated.
- **Adaptive dwell (b75f942)** — not in this build; nothing here evaluates it.
- **Delay underweighting / `OBS_BLEND_WEIGHT`** — the log carries no
  `delaySeconds` or schedule anchor, so the 0.75/0.25 blend cannot be isolated
  from projection error. R7 would fix this.
- **Model-specific tuning** — model differences (t3 vs 15t) are confounded
  with line assignment in an 11-minute window; no action supported.
- **Convergence dynamics of the EWMA** — the fleet entered the log already
  converged; only 96 fresh-sim fix events exist (enough to show the prior
  problem, not enough to tune half-life precisely).
- **AVL fix latency** — `obsDist` change times are quantized to the 5 s poll;
  true `observedAtMs` is not logged, so the ~55 s figure is refresh interval,
  an upper bound mixing provider latency and vehicle report rate.
- **11 minutes, one evening** — everything above is a Friday-night snapshot;
  rush-hour behavior (queueing, longer dwells, centre zone) is unmeasured.
