1.0, `expected × 1.0` bit-identical; full-suite green, replay identical by

future TOD entry cannot double-apply. Unit tests (tod-pace.test.ts ×3):
residual learning under a uniform 0.5 table (ratio 0.9, not 0.45), midpoint
hour-blend evaluation (07:10–07:20 span → factor at 07:15), neutral-table
regression guard. Jest 22 suites / **354 tests** green; `npx tsc --noEmit`
